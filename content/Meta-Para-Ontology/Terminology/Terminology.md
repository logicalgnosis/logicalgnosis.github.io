**Meta-Para-Ontology (MPO)**  
A structural meta-system for the integration of knowledge, providing a universal coordinate architecture for localizing philosophical, scientific, psychological, religious, and contemplative paradigms within a single ontological reality.

**Logical Gnosis**  
The experiential dimension of Meta-Para-Ontology, translating its structural architecture into a direct path of intellectual realization through which the Meta-Paradigm becomes experiential knowledge.

**Register Ontology** 
The architectonic component of Meta-Para-Ontology that reconstructs the four invariant ontological registers through which reality becomes describable.

**Functional Ontology**
The operational component of Meta-Para-Ontology that reconstructs the universal triad of functions through which Manifestation unfolds.

**Polymetaphysics**
The analytical discipline that studies reality through the structural localization and comparison of multiple ontological systems.

**Polymetaphysical Cartography**  
The methodological framework for localizing philosophical systems, scientific theories, psychological models, and contemplative traditions within one coherent Meta-System while preserving their internal conceptual integrity.

**Meta-System**
A higher-order framework capable of integrating multiple internally coherent paradigms while preserving their conceptual integrity.

**Ontology**
The domain of meaningful description extending from the emergence of articulation to the legitimate limits of describability.

**Trans-Ontological**
That which lies beyond ontology.

**Observational Pole (Invariant Coordinate Center)**  
Consciousness itself, as the invariant coordinate center of individual experience within Manifestation, without ever belonging to it.

**Pure Consciousness**  
The permanent witnessing principle, remaining entirely independent of Manifestation and every ontological determination.

**Involved Consciousness**
The ontological presence of Consciousness as the observational pole, accompanying Manifestation without belonging to it.

**Trans-Ontological Horizon**
The structural boundary marking the exact limit of the ontological contour, where conceptual description concludes.

**Trans-Ontological Domain**
The trans-ontological dimension of reality lying beyond the Trans-Ontological Horizon. It is the domain of Pure Consciousness—the intrinsic nature of the witnessing pole—which accompanies the entire ontological architecture while forever remaining beyond conceptual articulation.

___

**The Four Registers**  
The four invariant modes through which reality becomes describable, together constituting the complete ontological contour.

**Meta-Potentiality**
The first ontological register corresponding to pure openness preceding every determination.

**Formative Causality**
The second ontological register corresponding to formative limitation, organization, and pre-manifest structure.

**Manifestation**
The third ontological register corresponding to articulated, observable, and experiential reality.

**Meta-Actualization**
The fourth ontological register corresponding to the recognition of consciousness as irreducible to Manifestation.

___

**The Three Functions**
The triadic functional configuration consisting of the Recall, Differentiation, and Actualization Functions through which every phenomenon of Manifestation and experience becomes structurally organized.

**Recall Function**  
The universal operational dynamic generating continuity, persistence, and the causal function of time.

**Differentiation Function**  
The universal operational dynamic generating distinction, structure, and the causal function of space.

**Actualization Function**  
The universal operational dynamic generating emergence, novelty, and the causal function of evolution.

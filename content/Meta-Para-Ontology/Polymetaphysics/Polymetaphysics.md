Polymetaphysics is the methodological dimension of Meta-Para-Ontology. It investigates the structural role of existing philosophies and empirical disciplines by determining their precise ontological position within reality. Shifting the focus from truth-value judgments, it reconstructs the coordinates from which every system describes reality. Every coherent philosophy, scientific theory, religious metaphysics, or contemplative tradition observes reality as a particular ontological configuration. Because each occupies a distinct structural position, apparent contradictions naturally arise.

Polymetaphysics preserves these differences by mapping them within a shared ontological landscape while maintaining their distinct structural identities. Within this methodology, disagreement transforms into structural localization. Opposition becomes a matter of positional coordinates. Materialism, Idealism, Phenomenology, Process Philosophy, Religion, contemporary physics, psychology, and systems theory all become structurally intelligible as complementary coordinates.

Polymetaphysics identifies the precise domains where any given system remains structurally valid. This transforms philosophical inquiry. The task shifts from the production of competing ontologies to the reconstruction of the coordinate positions from which every ontology derives its internal coherence. Polymetaphysics achieves structural integration through precise localization, entirely bypassing the need for synthetic compromise.

Polymetaphysical Cartography executes this reconstruction through three complementary operations:

- **Functional Reconstruction** — reconstructs the invariant structural function performed by a concept independently of its historical terminology and disciplinary origin.
- **Cartographic Localization** — localizes the reconstructed structural function within the invariant ontological topology established by Register Ontology.
- **Structural Closure** — reveals the intrinsic explanatory boundary at which a philosophical system reaches the legitimate limit of its own structural resources.

Every coherent system therefore appears simultaneously as internally valid, structurally localized, methodologically complete, and ontologically limited.

The methodology is currently undergoing final testing before its public presentation as a complete operational procedure.


## Two Courses. One Architecture.
___
  
- ## [Course 1: Self-Guided Program in Logical Gnosis](/logical-gnosis/self-guided-program)
  A self-guided course for a deep understanding of reality.
  
---
  
- ## [Course 2: Self-Awareness & the Ontology of Belief](/logical-gnosis/self-awareness--and--the-ontology-of-belief)
  A practical meta-system for recognizing and reconstructing your own states, beliefs, and worldviews across different scales and paradigms.
  
---

Most systems describe Reality from one side only. They choose one side of longstanding philosophical oppositions: rigorous logic or metaphysics, materialism or idealism, science or religion, reason or revelation, rational analysis or contemplative experience, ontology or phenomenology. They defend one side against everything the other side cannot explain.

These two courses change the approach itself, expanding the boundaries of limiting paradigms and opening a new meta-systemic perspective.

The first course reconstructs the architecture from the ground up, resolving apparent contradictions by extending the boundaries of ontology to their actual limits. The second course takes that same architecture and turns it inward. Your beliefs, your states, your sense of self, your worldview — these are not exceptions to the structure of reality. They are part of the same unfolding architecture, accessible through the methods of Logical Gnosis. The ancient principle of macrocosm and microcosm is no longer an esoteric analogy, but receives a rigorous rational explanation.

The courses develop a meta-systemic perspective from which structures of reality can be examined, compared, and related. The result is a new position of witnessing within lived experience — a way of understanding reality precise enough for rigorous inquiry, and direct enough to be lived.

___

### Entry Threshold

The intellectual entry threshold is deliberately low. The reader does not need prior knowledge of:

* metaphysics;
* ontology;
* epistemology;
* phenomenology;
* philosophy of mind;
* Indian philosophy and metaphysics;
* Meta-Para-Ontology.

What is useful is the ability to pause and observe one's own experience, and to hold abstract distinctions in view. If these abilities are not yet developed, the courses themselves can serve as a training ground for developing them.

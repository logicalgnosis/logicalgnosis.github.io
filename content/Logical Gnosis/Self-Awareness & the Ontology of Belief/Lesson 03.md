## Functions as Metaphysical Constants

___
## Lesson 3

The three Functions are now established.

Recall.

Differentiation.

Actualization.

We have seen how they participate together in the formation of Manifestation.

Now we can ask a different question.

**What happens if one of them is absent?**

Let's begin with Recall.

### Without Recall

Without Recall, a previous configuration

could no longer participate

in the formation of a new moment.

But this immediately raises a deeper question:

**Could a new moment form at all?**

The question almost answers itself.

Its significance follows from the architecture

introduced in the first course.

But for now,

let us set aside the question

of the architecture of reality itself

and examine the problem

at the level of psychological experience.

Imagine that every new manifestation arrives

without access to anything previously formed.

A familiar face could be seen,

but nothing from previous encounters

could make it familiar.

A word could be heard,

but nothing learned before

could make its meaning available.

Everything that happened before

could no longer enter the new moment.

Without Recall,

a subsequent manifestation

cannot inherit anything previously formed.

Even if the present could still occur,

even if something could still be differentiated,

and even if something new could still become actual,

what has already been formed

could not become available again.

And with that,

continuity would be lost.

Because nothing previously formed

could carry forward

from one moment into another.

Now consider Differentiation.

### Without Differentiation

Suppose previously formed configurations

can still become available.

Suppose something genuinely new

can still become actual.

But nothing present

can become structurally distinct

from anything else.

The page cannot be distinguished from the desk.

One sound cannot be distinguished from another.

Foreground cannot separate from background.

A boundary cannot be established.

A relation cannot be identified.

There may still be something present,

but there is no clear **this rather than that**.

The problem is not that nothing exists.

The problem is that what is present

cannot acquire the distinctions

through which its contents become identifiable,

localized, and related.

Recall could make something available,

but there would be no differentiated structure

in which that configuration could be articulated.

Actualization could produce something new,

but the new configuration could not acquire

the distinctions through which

its structure could become clear.

Now consider Actualization.

### Without Actualization

Suppose previously formed configurations

can still become available,

and the present can still become differentiated.

Recall continues.

Distinction continues.

Relations can still be established.

But nothing genuinely new  

can emerge in experience.

A possibility can remain a possibility,

but it cannot become a new realized configuration.

Previously formed configurations

can return.

Present configurations

can become distinct and related.

But the process cannot produce

something that did not exist before.

The field can continue,

but nothing genuinely new

can enter it.

There can be continuity.

There can be distinction.

There can be relation.

But there can be no genuine formation

of novelty.

The process could continue

to reproduce what has already been formed

and to establish new relations

between existing configurations.

But it could not produce

a configuration that had never existed before.

The world would therefore remain

within the limits of what was already actual.

No new form of organization

could emerge from it.

No new form of life

could arise where life had not existed.

No new level of organization

could develop from what had already been formed.

Now look at what these three absences reveal.

Without **Recall**,

what has been formed

cannot become available again.

Without **Differentiation**,

what is present

cannot become structurally distinct and related.

Without **Actualization**,

nothing genuinely new

can become actual.

Each absence removes something

that the other two cannot simply provide.

And this gives us

a useful way to see the Functions

at a level beyond their immediate descriptions.

The first condition concerns

the continuation of what has already formed.

The second concerns

the distinction and articulation

of what is present.

The third concerns

the emergence of genuinely new configurations.

Now notice something.

These three conditions

are not unfamiliar.

We already have names for the dimensions

in which they become most apparent.

**Time.**

**Space.**

**Evolution.**

### Recall and Time

We have already seen

what disappears when Recall is absent.

Previously formed configurations

can no longer become available again.

Continuity is lost.

Now stay with that for a moment.

One manifestation occurs.

Then another.

Then another.

Suppose nothing from the first

can become available in the second.

Nothing from the second

can become available in the third.

Each manifestation occurs,

and then becomes structurally closed

to what follows.

Ask yourself:

**What is left of the relation between them?**

There is succession.

But is succession enough?

If something happened

and nothing from that event

could ever participate

in what follows,

what would it mean to say

that it happened **before**?

And if what is happening now

had no structural relation

to anything that happened before,

what would it mean to call it

the **present**?

Notice what the question is revealing.

We normally speak as though

the past already exists somewhere,

the present occupies another point,

and manifestations simply occur

inside an already existing Time.

But what would make

one manifestation genuinely

the past of another?

Not merely that it occurred earlier.

Something from it

must remain capable

of participating in what follows.

Something formed previously

must be able to carry forward.

And that is precisely

what Recall makes possible.

Recall therefore does more

than make previous configurations

available again.

It makes continuity possible.

And once continuity exists,

what has happened

can stand in a structural relation

to what is happening.

There can be a meaningful

**before**

and a meaningful

**after**.

Now ask:

**What is this relation

if not temporality?**

And what do we ordinarily call

the dimension in which

before and after

become possible?

**Time.**

Perhaps, then,

we have been starting

from the wrong direction.

Perhaps Time does not first exist

and then provide a container

for Manifestation.

Perhaps the possibility

of continuity comes first.

Perhaps temporality emerges

when what has been formed

can remain available

to what follows.

And perhaps what we call **Time**

is the dimensional expression

of that continuity.

Recall has therefore led us

beyond the immediate phenomenon

of something returning.

It has revealed a condition

under which Manifestation

can have a past,

a present,

and a meaningful relation

between them.

### Differentiation and Space

Now ask a similar question

of Differentiation.

Suppose two configurations

are present.

What makes them two?

Not simply the fact

that we have named them separately.

There must be some difference

through which one can be distinguished

from the other.

Some boundary.

Some relation.

Some structural distinction

that allows one configuration

to be this

rather than that.

Now push the question further.

If two configurations

are genuinely distinct,

what would it mean

to say that one is **here**

and another is **there**?

What makes here different from there?

Could there be a meaningful

**where**

without distinctions?

Could there be distance

without separation?

Could there be localization

without something being distinguished

from what surrounds it?

And if nothing could be distinguished

from anything else,

what would there be

to localize at all?

The question takes us

beyond ordinary perception.

Differentiation does not merely

allow us to notice

that one object differs from another.

It establishes the structural

distinctions through which

configurations can become

separate,

localized,

and related.

Now ask:

**What is the dimensional expression

of such differentiation?

What do we ordinarily call

the possibility of here and there,

of separation,

distance,

and spatial relation?

**Space.**

Perhaps Space, like Time,

has therefore been appearing

more fundamental than the process

through which its possibility arises.

We usually imagine configurations

already existing in Space.

But the possibility of a meaningful

where

depends first upon differentiation.

Space can therefore be understood

not as an empty container

waiting for configurations to enter,

but as a dimension arising

through the differentiation

by which configurations

become distinct,

localized,

and relationally ordered.

### Actualization and Evolution

Now there is one more question.

Suppose Recall continues.

Previously formed configurations

can become available again.

Suppose Differentiation continues.

The present field can become

increasingly articulated.

Configurations can differ.

Relations can be established.

But ask:

**Is anything genuinely new

possible yet?**

A previous configuration can return.

An existing configuration

can become more clearly differentiated.

An existing relation

can be reorganized.

But what if nothing

that was not previously formed

could ever become a new configuration?

Would there be genuine emergence?

Now imagine that something new

does become actual.

What happens then?

It is no longer merely

a possibility.

It has entered Manifestation.

It can now become part

of what is available

for further differentiation,

further Recall,

and further actualization.

Another possibility can emerge

from what has now become actual.

And then another.

Ask yourself:

**What is happening here?**

Is this merely a sequence

of different states?

Or is the field itself

becoming capable

of configurations

that were not available before?

Something has emerged.

That emergence changes

what can emerge next.

A new configuration

becomes part of the conditions

from which further configurations

can arise.

Now the word **Evolution**

begins to acquire

a more fundamental meaning.

Not merely the biological

development of organisms.

Not merely change over time.

But the capacity of Manifestation

to generate new configurations

from what has already

become actual,

so that what emerges

can itself become a condition

for further emergence.

Actualization therefore

does not merely add another event.

It opens the possibility

of cumulative novelty.

### The Functions Across Manifestation

Now notice what has happened.

We first encountered the three Functions

through ordinary experience.

Recall appeared as the return

of something previously formed.

Differentiation appeared

as distinction and relation.

Actualization appeared

as the emergence of something new.

But following their consequences

has taken us beyond psychology.

Recall does not merely explain

how a memory can return.

It provides the continuity

through which one manifestation

can remain related to another.

Differentiation does not merely explain

how the mind distinguishes objects.

It provides the structural distinction

through which configurations

can become distinct and related.

Actualization does not merely explain

how a new thought or response appears.

It provides the possibility

for genuinely new configurations

to emerge within Manifestation.

So the Functions cannot be understood

as exclusively psychological operations.

They belong to Manifestation itself.

Every particular manifestation

is organized through their operation:

what has already been formed

can become available,

what is present can become distinct

and relationally structured,

and what is possible

can become actual.

Psychological experience

is therefore not the place

where these Functions begin.

It is one place

where their operation becomes visible.

The Functions extend

through the entire field of Manifestation.

And now we can follow

what happens when this organization

continues from one manifestation

into another.

If a configuration can return,

can it begin to **persist**?

That is where we go next.

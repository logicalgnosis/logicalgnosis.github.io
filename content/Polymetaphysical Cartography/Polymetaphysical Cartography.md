Polymetaphysical Cartography is the operational framework of Meta-Para-Ontology. It provides the practical methodology required to reconstruct and localize the conceptual structures of individual disciplines—such as quantum physics, Indian metaphysics, phenomenology, evolutionary biology, psychology, and systems theory—within the unified topological architecture established by the four ontological Registers.

Applying the methodological principles of Polymetaphysics, Polymetaphysical Cartography reconstructs the invariant structural functions performed by concepts independently of their historical vocabulary or disciplinary origin, and localizes them within a common ontological coordinate system. In doing so, it preserves the internal conceptual integrity of every paradigm while revealing its precise structural position within the Meta-System.

Polymetaphysical Cartography identifies structural correspondence, moving beyond comparisons based on analogy, historical influence, or conceptual similarity. Philosophical systems, scientific theories, psychological models, and contemplative traditions become structurally comparable because the invariant operations they perform can be localized within the same ontological architecture.

Because the complete cartographic reconstruction of the world's knowledge systems represents a long-term research program, the interactive cartographic matrix remains under active development. This section will gradually become the central navigational interface for exploring structural correspondences across disciplines, while new mappings and methodological refinements are continuously added as the project evolves.
